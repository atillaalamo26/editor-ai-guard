## 🛡️ Overview

[**Editor AI Guard**](https://editor-ai-guard.com/) is an all-in-one academic integrity platform engineered for university students, graduate researchers, professors, and scientific authors. 

It solves the growing epidemic of unfair false-positive AI accusations by providing auditable linguistic metrics, an ethical citation-preserving humanizer, live academic plagiarism screening against 150M+ peer-reviewed papers, and cryptographic verification certificates.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

- **Multi-Metric AI Detection**: Measures statistical **Perplexity**, **Burstiness variance**, and **Entropy** with granular sentence-by-sentence highlights.
- **Academic Sentence Humanizer**: Ethically restructures mechanical phrasing while rigorously preserving scientific citations and research tone.
- **Live Scholarly Plagiarism Check**: Real-time cross-referencing against 150M+ peer-reviewed DOIs via Crossref REST API.
- **Verifiable QR Certificates**: Generates tamper-proof cryptographic authorship reports with scannable QR codes to objectively dispute false-positive AI flags.
- **Zero Data Retention**: Privacy-first serverless edge architecture (Cloudflare Workers) that never stores, leaks, or trains on submitted research.
- **13 Languages Supported**: Full native localization for English, Arabic, Spanish, French, German, Chinese, Japanese, Russian, Turkish, Portuguese, Italian, Korean, and Indonesian.
